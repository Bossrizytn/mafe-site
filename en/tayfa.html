CHAPTER 01 — HOW WE WORK

A few products, built end to end ourselves.

How we work

We make a small number of products and build each one ourselves, end to end: the design, the software and the AI.

Most of our budget goes into the product, not advertising. We don't try to grow a product until it genuinely does the job.

We talk directly to the people who will use our products: clinic coordinators, investors, people who train.

In areas like health and money we decide up front where the AI stops; a person always makes the final call.

We give a settings screen nobody opens the same care we give the home page.

If you have an idea or want to work with us, write to us.
