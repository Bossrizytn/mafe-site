EXHIBIT 03 — CONTROL

The assistant knows; the doctor decides.

The last word belongs to your doctors.

Your clinic draws the lines. The moment a question turns clinical, the conversation moves to your team, with a summary.

Goes to your team

  • Any clinical question: candidacy, risks, anaesthesia, medication
  • Possible emergencies
  • Anyone possibly under 18
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Revision or repair cases
  • Shared photos or medical history
  • Anyone who asks for a person
  • Anything it isn't sure about

Reserved for your doctors

  • No diagnosis, no grading
  • No graft estimates
  • No "you're a candidate"
  • No promised results
  • No medication or dosage advice
  • Photos go to your surgeon, not to a model
  • No prices without authorisation on file
